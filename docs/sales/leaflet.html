TreatmentTrack Built by UK dentists

Turn “I’ll think about it” into booked treatment.

TreatmentTrack follows up every treatment plan your practice quotes, with polite texts and emails in your practice’s name. It stops the moment the patient books or says no — and once they book, it tracks the case through to completion.

How it works

  1. 1
    Add your quotes

    Type one in, or import your unbooked treatment plans from Dentally, SOE, R4 or CareStack.

  2. 2
    It follows up for you

    Polite messages on a schedule you choose — e.g. 3 days, a week, a month — in your words. Patient says “after Christmas”? Pause it until then.

  3. 3
    Stops when they decide

    Mark it booked, declined or lost and messages stop straight away. Replies of STOP are honoured automatically.

  4. 4
    Track it to the final fit

    Booked patients move along a board for each treatment — consultation, consent, placement, scan, fit — and anyone overdue is flagged.
